# The Anonymous Family

**Privacy as Method** — a Simple Minds Studios research exhibit examining one Michigan family archive across migration, memory, evidence, and eleven generations without publishing a directory of living people.

**Live exhibit:** https://simple-minds-studios.github.io/the-anonymous-family/

## The question

> How much can we learn about a family without exposing the family?

The project treats privacy as part of the research method rather than as a redaction step performed at the end. The private working archive may retain names, exact relationships, source files, and working-tree material. The public edition publishes only what is needed to understand historical patterns, evidence strength, uncertainty, and archival absence.

## Public edition

The current exhibit presents:

- **375** people in the private working dataset
- **~121** direct ancestors in the research roadmap
- **11** generations at the deepest working lines
- **4** visible evidence confidence levels
- migration currents rather than public person-level lineage maps
- named deceased historical cases only where a public historical record is necessary to the analysis
- explicit separation between document-confirmed evidence, family-confirmed knowledge, working-tree leads, and family tradition

These figures describe the working research artifact and do not imply that every line is equally verified.

## Privacy boundary

The public repository and public exhibit deliberately exclude:

- living-person lineage nodes
- exact living-person dates
- private addresses or contact information
- raw GEDCOM files
- sensitive record identifiers
- unredacted personal documents
- direct public chains connecting the project steward to living relatives

See [`PRIVACY.md`](PRIVACY.md) for the publication rules.

## Version

**Public edition 2.0 · September 2026**

The artifact is intentionally versioned. Future editions may add aggregate maps, citation counts, redacted scans, occupational analysis, audio recollections, and newly verified generations without turning the living family into the interface.

## Rights and source material

No raw private genealogy dataset is published in this repository, and no open-data license is granted for private family source material. Public historical records retain the rights and restrictions of their original sources.
